Historical Monuments of India

Taj Mahal

An ivory-white marble mausoleum in Agra, built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

Qutub Minar

A UNESCO World Heritage Site in Delhi, this 73-meter tall minaret was constructed by Qutb-ud-din Aibak in 1192.

Gateway of India

A majestic arch in Mumbai, built in 1924 to commemorate the visit of King George V and Queen Mary to India.

Hawa Mahal

The "Palace of Winds" in Jaipur, known for its unique honeycomb-like structure with 953 small windows.

Red Fort

A historic fort in Delhi, built by Shah Jahan in 1648, which served as the main residence of Mughal emperors.
